Odoo 16 | UAE e-invoicing connector and integration

Explore the interface and workflows of the full Orchida UAE E-Invoicing Connector for Odoo. The production connector supports UAE FTA e-invoicing and e-invoice workflows in Odoo Accounting, validates UAE VAT invoice data, builds PINT AE structured payloads, submits eligible invoices and credit notes through the Orchida API, and tracks invoice status and responses in Odoo.

UAE FTA E-Invoicing Features

  • Automatic submission when eligible customer invoices and credit notes are posted.
  • Journal-level control with automatic or manual submission modes.
  • Pre-submission validation for customer data, invoice lines, taxes, payment details, emirates, and credit notes.
  • UAE e-invoice payload mapping with buyer information, VAT categories, unit codes, discounts, surcharges, and payment data.
  • Submission history with API responses, HTTP status, UUID, attempts, synchronization time, and readable error summaries.
  • Automatic status refresh for pending submissions, plus manual status checks, retries, and correction workflow.
  • Failure classification for document, configuration, and technical issues.

UAE VAT, RCM, Export and Credit Note Scenarios

  • Standard customer invoices and customer credit notes.
  • Credit-note reason codes and links to the original invoice.
  • Reverse charge (RCM) support with GTIN, NatureCode, HS, and SAC readiness checks.
  • Export invoices with delivery address and Incoterm validation.
  • Free trade zone and e-commerce supply indicators.
  • Document-level allowances and charges with reason codes and tax treatment.
  • UAE tax categories, exemption codes, and emirate-aware buyer address mapping.

Odoo Accounting E-Invoice Workflow

  • Company settings for the Orchida API base URL, API token, company ID, and automatic submission.
  • Customer e-invoice data including TRN/VAT, endpoint ID, scheme, agency information, tax scheme, and payment instruction.
  • Journal setting to include or exclude customer invoices from submission.
  • Product identifiers, HS codes, SAC codes, RCM data, and unit-of-measure mappings.
  • Invoice actions for submission, status review, retry, correction, and submission history.

Typical Workflow

  1. Configure the company, customer, tax, product, journal, and unit-code information.
  2. Post an eligible customer invoice or credit note.
  3. Validate the document and build the UAE e-invoice payload.
  4. Submit automatically or send manually from the invoice.
  5. Review the API response, status, UUID, attempts, and any required correction.

Installation

  1. Use the branch matching your Odoo version.
  2. Copy the module folder into an Odoo addons path.
  3. Update the Apps list and install Egypt E-Invoicing Connector for Odoo | Orchida | ETA.
  4. Open the E-Invoicing menu to review the available screens.
